Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 May 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects when you use our expense-tracking application, why we collect it, how we protect it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described below.

1. What Data We Collect

To run Spendly and give you a useful experience, we collect:

  • Account information — your name and email address, provided when you register.
  • Authentication data — a securely hashed version of your password (never the password itself).
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates, and notes you record in the app.
  • Usage data — basic technical signals such as page views, browser type, and approximate location, used to keep the Service reliable.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and maintain your Spendly account.
  • Display, organise, and summarise the expenses you log.
  • Improve the reliability, performance, and features of the Service.
  • Communicate important updates about your account or the Service.
  • Detect and prevent abuse, fraud, or unauthorised access.

We do not use your expense data to build advertising profiles or sell insights about your spending to anyone.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in secure, access-controlled systems. Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes, and connections to Spendly are protected with industry-standard encryption in transit. We follow reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, but no system is perfectly secure — please use a strong, unique password and notify us immediately if you suspect any unauthorised activity.

4. Third-Party Sharing Policy

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share data only in the limited situations below:

  • With trusted service providers (such as hosting or email delivery) strictly to operate the Service, under confidentiality obligations.
  • When required by law, court order, or a valid government request.
  • To protect the rights, safety, or property of Spendly, our users, or the public.
  • As part of a business transfer (such as a merger), in which case we will notify you in advance.

5. Cookies & Tracking

Spendly uses a small number of cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences such as your light/dark theme choice, and understand how the Service is used in aggregate. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. You can control or disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features of Spendly may not work correctly without them.

6. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Edit or correct your account details and expense entries at any time.
  • Delete individual records, or your entire account, from within the app.
  • Export a copy of your expense data in a portable format.

To exercise any right not directly available in the app, contact us using the details below.

7. Children's Privacy

Spendly is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete that information from our systems.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the Service, our practices, or applicable law. When we do, we will update the "Effective date" at the top of this page. Material changes will be communicated to you through the app or by email. Your continued use of Spendly after changes take effect means you accept the updated Policy.

9. Contact Information

Questions or requests about your privacy or this Policy? Reach the Spendly team at privacy@spendly.app.
